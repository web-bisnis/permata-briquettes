## Current product range

Our current range is coconut shell charcoal briquettes for shisha and hookah, offered in Platinum, Super Premium and Premium grades. The grades differ in ash content, burn time, moisture and other reference specifications. Review the product page before selecting a grade for your market.

[Compare the Three Grades](/en/products/coconut-charcoal-briquettes-for-shisha/)

## Specify the order before requesting a price

Tell us the grade, shape, size, packaging, destination and expected order volume. Ask about cube, finger, hexagonal, flat and dome formats. The exact grade-size combination and packing configuration will be confirmed for each order.

## Built around the buyer's workflow

We act as your supplier and commercial point of contact. PT Permata Bara Globalindo issues the quotation, invoice and sales contract for Permata Briquettes transactions. Product specifications, supporting documents and shipping terms are confirmed as part of the order discussion.

## Quality information you can review

Product data includes ash, moisture, fixed carbon, volatile matter and burn-time references. ISO 9001:2015, ROA, SHT, MSDS and Factory Audit documents are available for review. The ISO certificate and Factory Audit relate to the manufacturer, not to certification of PT Permata Bara Globalindo. Ask for the applicable document and current batch report when assessing an offer.

[See Quality & Documents](/en/quality-documents/)

## Order and shipping basics

The minimum order is one 20 ft container. Net weight depends on the product and packing configuration. EXW, FOB, CNF and CIF can be quoted. Semarang is the primary port of loading in the source data, with Surabaya and Jakarta as alternatives subject to confirmation.

## Send us the specifications you need.

Share your destination and preferred product configuration. We will confirm availability, documents, packing and commercial terms in a quotation.
