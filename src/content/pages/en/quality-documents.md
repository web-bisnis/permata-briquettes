## Product parameters

The grade data includes ash content, moisture, fixed carbon, volatile matter, ignition time, burn time and ash colour. Before an order is placed, the agreed specification should identify the applicable limits and how they are tested.

## Available documents

| Document | How it should be presented |
| --- | --- |
| ROA / Report of Analysis | Product or batch analysis. |
| SHT / Self-Heating Test | Transport-related test document. |
| MSDS | Product safety and handling document. |
| Company registration | PT Permata Bara Globalindo's legal documents. |

## Documents for shipment

Shipment documents may include a Bill of Lading, commercial invoice, packing list, Certificate of Origin and export declaration. Additional documents depend on the product, destination and carrier. The exact set will be listed in the offer or contract.

## Shipping classification

Transport classification and required safety documents will be confirmed against the applicable MSDS and carrier requirements for each shipment.

## Need to check a specific parameter?

Tell us the grade, destination and documents required by your team or carrier. We will identify the applicable report and any items that need confirmation.
