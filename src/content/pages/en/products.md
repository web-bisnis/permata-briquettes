## Coconut shell charcoal briquettes for shisha and hookah

The listed components are coconut shell charcoal, food-grade tapioca and water. Reference characteristics include odor-free burning and three grades with different ash, moisture and burn-time figures. Product suitability and final specifications must be checked against the applicable sample and sales contract.

### Platinum

Ash 1.6–1.9%; reference burn time 2 h 30 min; moisture 5% max; fixed carbon 80% min.

### Super Premium

Reference burn time 2 h+; moisture 6% max; fixed carbon 80% min.

### Premium

Ash 2.3–2.5%; reference burn time 1 h 30 min+; moisture 8% max; fixed carbon 75% min.

## Shapes and sizes

Listed formats include cube, finger, hexagonal, flat/brix and dome. Availability is not established for every grade-size combination. Send the dimensions you need for confirmation.

## Barbecue briquettes

Hexagonal briquettes made from hardwood mixed with coconut charcoal granule, for barbecue grills. Listed sizes are 50×50, 50×100 and 50×200 mm, packed in boxes averaging 10 kg.
